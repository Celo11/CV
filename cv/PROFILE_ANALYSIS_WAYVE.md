## 3. Where you appear to fit at Wayve

Wayve describes its work as driving intelligence that learns from data and scales across vehicles and locations. Your most relevant contribution would be assessing and integrating driving-system behaviour, not claiming to develop the underlying learning models.

These are role families to discuss with your contact, not verified current vacancies or a guarantee of eligibility:

1. **Validation, verification and simulation engineering:** Your clearest match, especially scenario design, test criteria, failure investigation and communicating findings.
2. **Systems and vehicle integration:** Relevant requirements, signal/interface and supplier experience, alongside real vehicle and simulation work.
3. **Customer-facing application or integration engineering:** A credible route where the role involves helping automotive partners use tools, investigate problems and agree technical actions.
4. **Technical leadership within those areas:** Supported by your one-year team example. Staff-level scope, organisation-wide ownership and formal management should be assessed against the actual job, not inferred from your current title.

Your source material does not establish experience in ML training, neural-network development, production C++ implementation or large-scale infrastructure ownership. Some Wayve roles may require these; the CV should not try to conceal that distinction.
