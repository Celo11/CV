## 1. 90-Second Introduction (Say it naturally, don't read)

"I'm Celal. I'm a mechanical and automotive engineer—I did my BSc in Mechanical Engineering and my MSc in Sustainable Automotive Engineering at WMG, University of Warwick—and I have eight years in automotive testing, validation and multi-discipline coordination. I live in Coventry, about 20 minutes from Leamington Spa.

My background started in powertrain and mechanical testing: sizing an electric bus powertrain at TEMSA, and designing a thermal model test for an electric motor stator for my Warwick dissertation, specifying the thermocouple placement, enclosure and test method in MotorCAD and Simulink. When I joined AVL Powertrain UK, a project needed people in ADAS, and I spent two years on-site at Jaguar Land Rover testing on dSPACE HiL and ViL rigs and on the proving ground. Alongside that, I worked on AVL's DrivingCube Vehicle-in-the-Loop dyno test bed—fitting an external GNSS system for position ground truth, driving airstrip and HORIBA MIRA tests to calibrate the simulation PID controller, and monitoring logging health during runs—and at the LEVC factory I ran multimeter and bench-supply checks on returned range-extender generators.

For the last five years at FiveAI, now part of Bosch, my role moved into test software development, where I led a team of five engineers for a year and acted as the first point of contact for Volkswagen Group partner engineers across disciplines. Following Volkswagen Group's withdrawal from the project, my role at Bosch ended by redundancy, so I am immediately available. Now I want to get back to physical test facilities, rigs and hardware, which is why this role at Ricardo in Leamington Spa really appeals to me."
